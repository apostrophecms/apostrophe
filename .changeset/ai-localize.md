Add AI localization (proof of concept). When `@apostrophecms/ai` has a provider configured, the Localize dialog asks "What do you want to localize?" and defaults to "Only new and modified content": an AI agent brings each locale's copy up to date with what changed in the source since it was last localized, keeping edits made in that locale alone, while documents new to a locale are copied in full. The "Translate text content" checkbox is offered in this mode whether or not automatic translation is installed: checked, what is carried over is translated; unchecked, it goes in as the source has it, for translating later. Related documents follow the same policy. The dialog shows the agent's progress as it works, can stop it, and ends with the usual localization notification. Localizing back the other way (for instance fr → en after editing the French copy directly) compares from the last localization in either direction. A module can offer the agent a translation service by registering an AI tool tagged `ai-localize-translate`; the agent then leaves the wording to it, otherwise it translates on its own. The new `@apostrophecms/ai-localize` module registers the agent's tools with `apos.ai` under the `ai-localize` tag and keeps a record of each localization, written by AI passes and by the classic Localize flow alike.
