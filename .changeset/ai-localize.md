Add AI localization (proof of concept). When `@apostrophecms/ai` has a provider configured, the Localize dialog asks "What do you want to localize?" and defaults to "Only new and modified content": an AI agent brings each locale's copy up to date with what changed in the source since it was last localized, keeping edits made in that locale alone, while documents new to a locale are copied in full. The "Translate content" checkbox (renamed from "Translate text content") is offered whenever automatic translation or AI is available, for either choice: checked, what is carried over is translated; unchecked, it goes in as the source has it, for translating later. With "All content", automatic translation still does the whole job when installed; without it, AI copies each document whole and translates it. Related documents follow the same policy. Localize Content now moves to a Localizing step, for every way of localizing a single document: one phrase at a time while it runs, then a one-line summary with the stages collapsed under "Localized in …". The dialog stays open while it runs, and Cancel stops it at once. Failures are listed by document and locale and never reported as success, and a page whose parent page is missing from a chosen locale is stopped before anything changes, with a message saying to localize the parent page first. Done ends with the usual localization notification. Related documents that were already up to date are named while they are checked but not in the result. Localizing back the other way (for instance fr → en after editing the French copy directly) compares from the last localization in either direction. A module can offer the agent a translation service by registering an AI tool tagged `ai-localize-translate`; the agent then leaves the wording to it, otherwise it translates on its own. The new `@apostrophecms/ai-localize` module registers the agent's tools with `apos.ai` under the `ai-localize` tag and keeps a record of each localization, written by AI passes and by the classic Localize flow alike.
